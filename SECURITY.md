# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **support@opengrants.io** with a description, reproduction steps and the affected version (shown in every page footer). Don't open a public issue.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days and to publish an advisory with the fix. The threat model and controls are in [docs/security.md](docs/security.md).
